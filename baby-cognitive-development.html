Baby Cognitive Development: A Guide for Parents

Cognitive development refers to the skills and processes involved in thinking, learning, problem-solving, and memory. During the first year, babies start to develop skills they will use for the rest of their lives . There is a wide range of what is considered &quot;normal,&quot; and your baby may be ahead in some areas and slightly behind in others .

Cognitive Milestones by Age

0Months

Your baby is learning through their senses. They can:

Recognize your voice and face.

Follow moving objects with their eyes.

Start to show interest in their surroundings.

4Months

Your baby is becoming more interactive and aware. They can:

Explore objects by putting them in their mouth and shaking them.

Reach for and grasp toys.

Begin to understand cause and effect (e.g., shaking a rattle makes noise).

7Months

Your baby is developing object permanence—the understanding that objects continue to exist even when they are out of sight. They can:

Look for hidden objects.

Copy simple actions, such as clapping or waving.

Show interest in cause and effect, such as dropping a toy to see what happens.

10Months

Your baby is becoming a more sophisticated thinker. They can:

Use objects correctly, such as drinking from a cup.

Follow simple directions.

Start to use objects in play, such as pretending to talk on a phone.

How to Support Cognitive Development

Talk to your baby frequently, narrating what you are doing.

Read simple board books and point to pictures.

Sing songs and play games like &quot;peek-a-boo.&quot;

Provide safe toys that are interesting to touch, shake, and explore.

Allow your baby time to explore and practice reaching for objects.

When to Talk to Your Pediatrician

If you are worried about possible delays, talk to your baby&#x27;s health care provider . A healthcare professional can assess your baby&#x27;s development and recommend early intervention if needed.
