Cognitive Development in Your 9-Month-Old: What to Expect

Cognitive development refers to the skills and processes involved in thinking, learning, problem-solving, and memory. At 9 months old, your baby is becoming more aware of their environment and is learning to interact with the world in more complex ways.

Cognitive Milestones at 9 Months

At 9 months old, your baby can typically:

Understand object permanence, knowing that objects continue to exist even when they are out of sight.

Look for hidden objects and may try to retrieve them.

Copy simple actions, such as clapping or waving.

Show interest in cause and effect, such as dropping a toy to see what happens.

Recognize familiar people and objects and may show recognition of specific words.

Respond to simple requests, such as &quot;come here.&quot;

Explore objects by shaking, banging, and dropping them.

How to Support Cognitive Development

You can encourage your baby&#x27;s cognitive growth through everyday play and interactions:

Play peek-a-boo to reinforce object permanence.

Provide toys that encourage cause-and-effect exploration.

Read simple board books and point to pictures.

Sing songs and play games like &quot;pat-a-cake.&quot;

Talk to your baby frequently, narrating what you are doing.

Encourage your baby to explore and play independently.

When to Talk to Your Pediatrician

Babies do not develop at the same rate, but it is always best to trust your instincts. If you have concerns about your baby&#x27;s development, talk to your pediatrician. Developmental and behavioral screening tests are available, and your doctor can monitor your child&#x27;s progress and address any delays early on.
