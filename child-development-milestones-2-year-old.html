Child Development Milestones: Your 2-Year-Old

Understanding Typical Development

By age 2, children typically reach key milestones across four main areas: social/emotional, language/communication, cognitive, and physical development . At this age, a child may notice when others are hurt or upset, such as pausing or looking sad when someone is crying, which is an important social/emotional milestone . They also begin to look at you to figure out how to react in new situations .

Language and Communication

A 2-year-old's language skills grow rapidly. Most children can say at least two words together (e.g., "more milk") and point to at least two body parts when asked . They may use more gestures, such as blowing a kiss or nodding yes . By 24 months, many children speak about 50 words, and are understood by caregivers at least half the time .

Cognitive Development

Cognitive milestones involve learning and problem-solving. A 2-year-old can hold something in one hand while using the other (e.g., opening a container) , try to use switches or buttons on toys , and play with more than one toy at the same time, like putting toy food on a plate . Many also begin simple pretend play, such as talking on a toy telephone .

Physical Development

In movement, a 2-year-old can run and kick a ball . They may walk up a few stairs and use a spoon with some success . Most children at this age need 11 to 14 hours of sleep per day, including naps . Remember, children develop at different rates. If you have concerns, talk to your child's doctor .
